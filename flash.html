Frequency Watch firmware

For the LilyGO T-Watch S3. Installs from this page over USB; no software needed.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux).
  2. Turn the watch on and plug it into the computer with a data USB cable.
  3. Click the button, pick the watch's port from the list, then click Install.
This browser can't install firmware. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

No port listed? Try another USB cable (many are charge-only), or hold the side button for 6 seconds to turn the watch off, then plug it in and try again.

This replaces LilyGO's factory demo. LoRa, WiFi and Bluetooth stay off. Tones of 40 Hz and up play through the speaker; lower ones pulse the vibration motor. This is an audio/vibration tool, not a medical device.
