    Most phone speakers and earbuds reproduce roughly 50 Hz – 18 kHz; tones outside that range may be silent. Keep volume moderate — long exposure to pure tones can strain hearing. The Rife labels show what community lists (mainly the CAFL) claim each frequency is for. None of these claims has scientific evidence behind it. This is an audio tool, not a medical device.
